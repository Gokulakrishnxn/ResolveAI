## Long description

> Most support tickets are the same five questions. ResolveAI answers
> them so your team can focus on the real ones.

### What it does
- **Auto-resolves "where's my order"** in seconds. Pulls live tracking
  from Shopify and replies in your brand voice — across email, website
  chat, and WhatsApp.
- **Auto-refunds, safely.** A built-in rules engine + fraud guards keep
  the auto-approve lane narrow (under $50, within 30 days, no abuse
  history) and route everything else to a one-click human review.
- **One inbox for every channel.** Email, chat, and WhatsApp messages
  show up in a single ticket view with AI confidence, draft reply, and
  full audit trail.
- **Knows your store.** Drop your shipping/refund/return policies in
  once and the AI cites them in every reply.
- **Pays for itself.** Most stores see a 60-80% auto-resolution rate
  in the first week and save the equivalent of one full-time agent
  per 1,000 monthly tickets.

### How it works
1. Click **Add app** — connect Shopify in 30 seconds.
2. Connect your support inbox (Gmail, Outlook, IMAP) — 90 seconds.
3. Pick an automation level: Conservative, Balanced, or Aggressive.
4. Optional: paste your help-center URL and we auto-import policy docs.
5. ResolveAI starts answering tickets within 5 minutes.

### What you stay in control of
- **Approval rules.** Set the dollar/age/reason thresholds for
  auto-refunds. Anything else needs a human click.
- **Tone & policies.** Edit the AI's voice and answer rules in plain
  English — no prompt engineering.
- **Audit log.** Every AI decision and refund is signed, timestamped,
  and linked to the source data. Export to CSV any time.

## Privacy / Data

- Customer PII is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and scrubbed after

## Permissions requested
- `read_orders`, `write_orders` — to fetch order status and issue refunds.
- `read_customers` — to look up the right order for an email address.
- `read_products` — to ground replies in your real catalog.
- `read_fulfillments` — for live tracking.
