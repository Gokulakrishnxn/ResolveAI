# Cold email — Shopify CX leads

**Audience:** CX / Ops leads at DTC Shopify stores doing $1M–$50M GMV.
**Sender:** Founder, from `sam@resolveai.app` (warmed sender, SPF/DKIM
clean, < 50/day per inbox).
**Goal:** Book a 20-minute "see it on your store" call.
**Reply rate target:** 8% on a clean list.

---

## Subject lines (test these)

1. "your support backlog → 9-second reply" (winner in beta tests)
2. "{First name}, two minutes on auto-refunds for {Store name}?"
3. "saw {Store name} on Shopify — quick CX question"

## Body (template)

> Hey {first_name},
>
> I noticed {Store name} is on Shopify and selling {category}.
> Quick guess: about 60–70% of the support tickets coming in are
> "where's my order?" and refund requests for items that arrived late
> or broken — am I close?
>
> I'm Sam, founder of ResolveAI. We just shipped v1.0 of an AI that
> auto-resolves those tickets in seconds — pulling live tracking from
> Shopify, drafting the reply in your brand voice, and (with rules
> you set) issuing safe refunds without a human click.
>
> One of our beta stores went from a 47-min median reply time to 9s,
> and saved their CX agent ~380 hours in the first month. Happy to
> show you what it looks like on **{Store name}**'s actual ticket
> patterns — should take 20 minutes, no slides.
>
> Free this Thursday or Friday afternoon?
>
> — Sam
> resolveai.app

## Notes for the SDR / founder running this

- **Personalize** the {category} and a single observation about the
  store from a recent IG post or product page. Not the whole site.
  Authenticity > volume.
- Cap at **3 emails per prospect**, then move on.
- Reply triage: anyone who says "we use Gorgias / Intercom / Zendesk"
  → reply with a one-liner about per-ticket pricing + offer a side-by-
  side. Don't dunk on the incumbent.
- Anyone who says "no" → log in CRM with reason, schedule re-touch in
  6 months.

## Compliance
- CAN-SPAM: include physical address in footer, one-click unsubscribe
  in footer.
- GDPR: only email B2B contacts with a publicly listed work address
  AND a clear legitimate-interest statement; honor unsubscribe within
  24 hours.
