## First comment (founder)

Hey Hunters! 👋

I'm Sam, founder of **ResolveAI**.

I spent four years on a CX team at a DTC brand. We had 12,000 tickets
a month. **The same five questions.** Every single time.

So we built ResolveAI:

- It reads your incoming email / chat / WhatsApp
- Looks up the order in Shopify
- Writes the reply itself, in your voice, with the live tracking
- Logs every decision with a signed audit trail

And for the harder stuff — refund requests — there's a rules engine
with built-in fraud guards. Under $50, within 30 days, no abuse history?
Auto-approved. Anything else? One-click human review.

**What's different from "another AI chatbot":**
- It's grounded in **your real Shopify data**, not generic knowledge.
- It auto-refunds — most "AI support" tools won't touch the money.
- The audit log is hash-chained for SOC2 readiness on day one.
- Per-ticket pricing. No "talk to sales" bullshit.

**Pricing:**
- Starter $29 / 500 tickets
- Growth $99 / 2,500 tickets
- Scale $299 / 10,000 tickets
- 14-day free trial — no credit card required.

If you run a Shopify store and you're drowning in support, give it a
spin: [resolveai.app](https://resolveai.app)

Happy to answer anything in the comments. The team is here all day.

— Sam
