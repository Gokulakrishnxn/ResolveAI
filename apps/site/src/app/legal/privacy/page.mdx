# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 27, 2026_

This is a placeholder Privacy Policy. Before launch, replace with a final version reviewed by counsel. The structure below covers what merchants and end-users typically expect.

## 1. Who we are

ResolveAI, Inc. (&quot;ResolveAI&quot;, &quot;we&quot;, &quot;us&quot;) operates the ResolveAI customer support platform.

## 2. Data we collect

- **Merchant account data** — name, email, business name (collected directly from you).
- **Integration data** — Shopify store URL, order &amp; customer records, support emails (collected via OAuth / IMAP / WhatsApp Cloud API).
- **End-customer data** — names, emails, message contents — only as needed to deliver support replies.
- **Operational data** — usage events for billing, audit logs of automated decisions.

## 3. Why we collect it

To provide the service: classify tickets, look up orders, draft replies, execute approved actions, and bill you.

## 4. Where it lives

Postgres (encrypted at rest) hosted on AWS-backed infrastructure. We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties for marketing.

## 5. How long we keep it

Messages: 90 days by default (configurable per merchant). Audit logs: 7 years for SOC2 compliance. Subscription &amp; billing records: as required by tax law.

## 6. Sub-processors

OpenAI (LLM inference), Stripe (billing), Sentry (error reporting), PostHog (product analytics — opt-out available), AWS (infrastructure).

## 7. Your rights

Merchants can request export or deletion of all data via support@resolveai.app. End-customers should contact the merchant directly; we will assist on request.
