# Data Processing Addendum

_Last updated: April 27, 2026_

This DPA forms part of the agreement between ResolveAI, Inc. (&quot;Processor&quot;) and the merchant (&quot;Controller&quot;) using the ResolveAI platform.

## 1. Roles

Controller determines purposes and means of processing. Processor processes personal data on Controller&apos;s behalf.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

Processing personal data of Controller&apos;s end customers (names, emails, message bodies, order references) for the purpose of automated customer support. Duration matches the underlying subscription.

## 3. Categories of data subjects

End customers of the merchant.

## 4. Sub-processors

- OpenAI (LLM inference, US)
- Stripe (billing, US)
- AWS (infrastructure, US/EU)
- Sentry (error monitoring, US)
- PostHog (product analytics, US — disabled on request)

Controller is notified of new sub-processors with at least 30 days&apos; notice and may object.

## 5. Security measures

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
- Per-tenant logical isolation, AES-256-GCM-wrapped integration credentials.
- Tamper-evident audit log signing.
- SOC2 Type II audit underway; controls available on request.

## 6. International transfers

Standard Contractual Clauses are executed for data transfers between EU and US entities.

## 7. Sub-processor list and updates

Available at https://resolveai.app/security#sub-processors. Updated whenever the list changes.

## 8. Data subject rights

Processor will assist Controller in responding to data subject requests (access, deletion, portability) within 30 days.
